FOCUS / SYSTEMSMODE / BUILD + DEBUG

I'm a technology enthusiast, and I'd rather build a system than just use one. Lately that's meant running a self-hosted home lab, poking at networking infrastructure, deploying local AI models, and putting together AI agents that can actually use tools.

What I keep coming back to is what's happening underneath the application — the servers, the network, the decision logic that quietly makes the thing work or not.
