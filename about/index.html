AYRON JINS / INDEPENDENT TECHNICAL WORK

FOCUS / SYSTEMSMODE / BUILD + DEBUG

I'm a technology enthusiast, and I'd rather build a system than just use one. Lately that's meant running a self-hosted home lab, poking at networking infrastructure, deploying local AI models, and putting together AI agents that can actually use tools.

What I keep coming back to is what's happening underneath the application: the servers, the network, the decision logic that quietly makes the thing work or not.

Reference / Quick facts

Ayron Jins at a glance.

Short factual answers to the questions people and search systems ask most often. Each one links to the page that documents it.

Who is Ayron Jins?
Ayron Jins is a student systems builder who works across AI agents, computer networking, Linux infrastructure, virtualization and self-hosted services. He designs, operates and documents the systems himself.
What is his website?
ayron.in is his official personal website and the primary source for his projects and technical writing.
What does he build?
An agentic computer-operation project called KHS AI, a self-managed homelab and network, a read-only infrastructure dashboard, a weekly technology news site, and a set of browser and server engineering tools.
What is KHS AI?
KHS AI is Ayron Jins's experimental agentic computer-operation project. It examines whether a language model can reason about a goal, use explicitly provided tools, observe the resulting state and verify outcomes while staying inside visible permission boundaries. Read the KHS AI case study.
Who created KHS AI?
Ayron Jins created KHS AI. It is a current exhibition project, documented at architecture and design level rather than as a deployed product.
What is his homelab?
A self-managed environment running Proxmox VE virtualization, Ubuntu Server hosts, Docker containers, an Nginx reverse proxy, DNS with AdGuard Home, TLS termination and firewall policy. See the homelab and networking case study.
What does he know about networking?
DNS resolution, IP addressing and subnetting, NAT and port forwarding, reverse proxies, HTTPS and TLS, firewall policy, and tracing a request end to end. Documented on the networking page.
What does he know about AI?
AI agents and controlled tool use, local model deployment with Ollama and Open WebUI, retrieval-augmented generation, and the engineering boundary between a model suggesting an action and a system performing one. Documented on the AI and development page.
Are his tools publicly usable?
Yes. The Tools Lab hosts a domain, DNS and TLS inspector, a browser-local subnet and VLSM planner, and a website metadata inspector. They run against a bounded backend and retain no scan history.
How can he be contacted?
By email at Ayronjins4@gmail.com. Professional profile: LinkedIn.
