About this website.

A clear record of ownership, purpose and publishing standards.

Owner and publisher

ayron.in is my personal engineering portfolio. It exists to document technical projects, systems work, ongoing learning and the practical decisions behind them.

Editorial standard

I write and maintain everything on this site myself. Completed work, current projects, conceptual diagrams and learning-stage material are labeled as such wherever the distinction matters. If you spot a factual error, email me and I'll fix it.

Technical delivery

The site runs over HTTPS on a static, semantic HTML architecture, so public pages can be indexed without JavaScript. Security headers, a sitemap, a robots policy and structured data are all published for browsers and search engines alike.

Security contact

Found a security issue? Email a responsible disclosure to Ayronjins4@gmail.com. The machine-readable policy lives at /.well-known/security.txt.
